Kryptos 2.3

* Panic password: a second password that wipes everything instead of unlocking.
* App passcode, for unlocking without biometrics.
* Key backup: your keys, contact keys and PGP keys in one password protected file.
* Argon2id everywhere, PBKDF2 removed.
* Photo steganography rebuilt: no visible signature, keyed adaptive placement.
* On-screen decryption finds messages in real messengers again.
* Password protected messages and stego photos from older versions no longer open.
